Privacy Policy

PersonalClipper · Effective November 19, 2026

Who this covers

PersonalClipper is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to YouTube channels the owner controls. It has no other users.

What Google data it accesses

  • YouTube upload access (youtube.upload): to upload videos and set their thumbnails.
  • YouTube read-only access (youtube.readonly): to read the channel name and ID and the status of uploaded videos, so each upload goes to the right channel.

It doesn't access email, contacts, files, or any other Google data.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to upload and check videos on the owner's own channels.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

PersonalClipper's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

PersonalClipper uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's third-party access page. Deleting the stored tokens from the owner's computer also ends access.
